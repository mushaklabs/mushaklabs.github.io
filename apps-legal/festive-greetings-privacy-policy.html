Privacy Policy — My Festive Greetings

Effective: October 13, 2026

My Festive Greetings does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works fully offline — your cards, messages, photos, videos, voiceovers, and settings are saved only on your device and never leave it.

We use no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see, access, or share anything about you because there is nothing to see.

Photos and videos you choose for a card background, and voiceovers you record, stay on your device. The app accesses your photo library and microphone only when you tap those features, and only to create the card you asked for.

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to retain, delete, or revoke. If you contact us at hello@mushaklabs.com with a question, we will only use your message to reply to you.
