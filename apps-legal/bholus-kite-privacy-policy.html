Privacy Policy for Bholu's Kite

Effective: November 3, 2026

Bholu's Kite does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works fully offline — your best score, gold-kite count, character choice, and settings are saved only on your device and never leave it.

We use no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see, access, or share anything about you because there is nothing to see.

If you contact us at hello@mushaklabs.com with a question, we will only use your message to reply to you.
