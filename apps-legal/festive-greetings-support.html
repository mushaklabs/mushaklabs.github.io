Support — My Festive Greetings

A greeting-card maker for iPhone and iPad: create beautiful Hindu festival greeting cards for Diwali, Navratri, Dussehra, Holi, Janmashtami, and everyday occasions — fully offline.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a card?

Pick a scene — a deity scene like Lakshmi, or Create Your Own. Type your message, add your signature, then tap Image, Video, or GIF to export and share it.

What is the Festive Art Gallery?

A collection of 51 curated Diwali artworks organized by theme — diyas, rangoli, marigold, fireworks, origami, festive lights, and celebrations. Tap any artwork to use it as your card background.

Can I use my own photo or video?

Yes. In Create Your Own, tap Upload Photo or Video and pick a photo or a video clip of 10 seconds or less from your library. It becomes the card background.

How does voiceover work?

Tap Record Voiceover and speak a message of up to 30 seconds. It plays in your shared video, with the music turned down underneath it. Image and GIF exports are silent.

What is the Wish Library?

A library of 164 heartfelt wishes in 12 languages, matched to the deity and occasion. Tap any wish to insert it into your message.

How do I share my card?

Tap Image for a still picture, Video for a 10-second video, or GIF for a looping animation. You can also export video in landscape (1920×1080).

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Everything is created on your device and the app works fully offline.

What data does My Festive Greetings collect?

None. No account, no analytics, no ads. See our Privacy Policy.
