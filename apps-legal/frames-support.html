Frequently asked questions

How do I open the settings?
Press any button on the Siri Remote (up, down, left, right, or the clickpad center) to reveal the gear button in the bottom-right corner, then click the center of the clickpad to open settings. The gear hides itself after 5 seconds.

How do I add my own photos and videos?
Apple TV does not let apps browse your photo library, so Festive Frames uses a simple upload page instead. Open settings and choose the option to add your own photos — your TV shows a QR code and a web address. On your phone or computer, connected to the same Wi-Fi network, scan the code or type the address, then pick up to 5 photos or videos at a time. They appear in the app right away. Photos can be JPG or PNG; videos can be MP4, MOV, or M4V, and videos longer than 15 seconds are trimmed to the first 15 seconds.

How many of my photos and videos can I keep in the app?
The library holds up to 30 uploads. When it is full, adding a new one removes the oldest to make room — the photo or video currently on screen is never removed.

How do I remove an uploaded photo or video?
In the library, long-press its tile and select the Remove option.

What is a "living portrait"?
A portrait plays once, then rests on its final frame while living effects — falling petals, twinkles, candlelight, a sweep of light — play over it, then stirs and plays again. Still photos use the pause timer you choose in settings. The app comes with five ready-made sample portraits; everything else is yours to frame.

Does Festive Frames need an internet connection?
No. The app works fully offline. Adding your own photos uses only your home Wi-Fi, directly between your phone or computer and your Apple TV — nothing is sent anywhere online.

What data does Festive Frames collect?
None. No account, no analytics, no ads. See our Privacy Policy.
