Privacy Policy for Festive Frames

Effective: October 21, 2026

Festive Frames does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works fully offline — your chosen frame, finish, effects, and settings are saved only on your Apple TV and never leave it.

The photos and videos you add are yours alone. They travel directly from your phone or computer to your Apple TV over your own home Wi-Fi network and are stored only on your Apple TV. They are never uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot see or access them.

We use no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see, access, or share anything about you because there is nothing to see.

If you contact us at hello@mushaklabs.com with a question, we will only use your message to reply to you.
