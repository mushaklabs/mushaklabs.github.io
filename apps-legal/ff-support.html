Frequently asked questions

How do I open the settings?
Press any button on the Siri Remote (up, down, left, right, or the clickpad center) to reveal the gear button in the bottom-right corner, then click the center of the clickpad to open settings. The gear hides itself after 5 seconds.

How do I change scenes?
Open settings, pick a scene from the list, or filter by collection (Fireplaces, Cats, Diwali, Christmas, and more). Your choice is remembered between launches.

How do I exit the app?
Press the Back button (<) on the Siri Remote while watching a scene. While the settings panel is open, Back closes the panel instead; press Done or Back again to return to your scene first.

Can I mix my own sounds?
Yes — open settings, choose Customize Sounds for the current scene, and layer any of the 25 ambient sounds (crackling fire, rain, thunder, crickets, festive music…) each with its own volume. Your custom mix is saved per scene. Reset restores the scene's original mix.

Does Fireplace & Festivals need an internet connection?
No. All videos and sounds are bundled with the app and everything works fully offline.

What data does Fireplace & Festivals collect?
None. No account, no analytics, no ads. See our Privacy Policy.
