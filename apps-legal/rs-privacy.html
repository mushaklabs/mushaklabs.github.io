Privacy Policy — Rangoli Studio

Effective: October 11, 2026

Rangoli Studio does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works fully offline — your designs, gallery, and settings are saved only on your device and never leave it, except through the share sheet when you explicitly choose to share or export something.

We use no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see, access, or share anything about you because there is nothing to see.

If you contact us at hello@mushaklabs.com with a question, we will only use your message to reply to you.
