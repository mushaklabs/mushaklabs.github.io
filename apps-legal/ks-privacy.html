KeySprint — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

KeySprint does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No data collected. The app gathers no personal information, usage analytics, diagnostics, or identifiers of any kind.
  • Fully offline. KeySprint makes no network connections. All typing results, profiles, settings, and history are stored locally on your device only.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. There is no sign-up, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.
  • Children's privacy. Kids Mode is designed for young learners and collects nothing — there is no data to protect because none is gathered.

If you delete the app, all locally stored data is removed with it.
