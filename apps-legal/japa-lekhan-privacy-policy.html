Privacy Policy for Japa Lekhan

Effective: November 16, 2026

Japa Lekhan does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works fully offline — your bead and mala counts, journal history, written pages, custom mantras, and settings are saved only on your device and never leave it.

We use no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see, access, or share anything about you because there is nothing to see.

If you contact us at hello@mushaklabs.com with a question, we will only use your message to reply to you.
