Bholu's Kite — Support

A free endless-runner chase across village rooftops. Pick your runner — Bholu, Tom, Rocky, or Bablu — sprint, jump, and double-jump across terraces while the kite dances just ahead. Fully offline. No ads, no purchases, no accounts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I jump?
Tap anywhere on the screen (or press Space / the Up arrow on a keyboard). Tap again mid-air to double-jump.

How do I catch the kite?
Just keep running. The kite stays just ahead of you — after enough play it slows down, Bholu catches up to it, and the catch triggers a confetti celebration where your kite turns to solid gold.

What happens after a catch?
You get a gold-kite celebration scene, then a "You did it!" screen showing how many gold kites you've collected. Tapping Start New Chase takes you back home, and your next chase starts with a brand-new kite colour.

How do I change characters?
Open Settings (gear button on the home screen) and pick Bholu, Tom, Rocky, or Bablu.

What is Daily Rooftops?
A special course with the same layout for everyone, all day — it changes at midnight. Find it on the home screen.

Does Bholu's Kite need an internet connection?
No. Everything is bundled with the app and it works fully offline.

What data does Bholu's Kite collect?
None. No account, no analytics, no ads. Your best score, gold-kite count, and settings are stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
