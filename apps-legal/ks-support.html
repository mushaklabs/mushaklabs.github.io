KeySprint — Support

KeySprint is a typing speed & accuracy practice app for Mac and iPad, with a Kids Racing Mode for young learners.

Practice Mode

  • Choose from 24 practice texts (20 prose passages + 4 drills).
  • Pick a duration: 15, 30, 60, or 120 seconds. The timer starts on your first keystroke.
  • Results show WPM, accuracy, characters, and keystrokes. Personal bests are tracked per profile.

Kids Mode

  • 10 lessons × 5 text variations, from "Find F & J" to full sentences.
  • Pick a world, pick a racer, and type to make them zoom. Finishing lessons earns stars and badges.
  • Music and Sound effects have separate toggles; 4 sound packs to choose from.

Profiles & History

  • Create multiple profiles (each with its own emoji); every profile's data is fully isolated.
  • History shows personal bests and recent tests, including kids lessons tagged separately.
  • Use "Clear history" to wipe a profile's test records.

FAQ

Does KeySprint need the internet?
No. Everything works fully offline.

Does KeySprint collect my data?
No. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking — all data stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Which devices are supported?
Mac (macOS 14+) and iPad (iPadOS 17+).
