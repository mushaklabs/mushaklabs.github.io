Support — Rangoli Studio

Draw beautiful rangolis on marble, glass, and transparent floors: freehand drawing with symmetry, stamps, diyas, preset designs, and a step-by-step illustrated guide — fully offline.

Frequently asked questions

What are floors?

The surface your rangoli sits on: Gray Marble, White Marble, Glass, or Transparent. Transparent is handy for overlays — export the PNG and drop it onto greeting cards or photos.

What are preset rangolis?

Thirteen hand-designed starting points (Rainbow Mandala, Peacock Garden, Royal Lotus, and more). Tap one to load it onto the canvas, then make it your own — or tap "See step-by-step instructions" to rebuild it ring by ring.

What does "Surprise me" do?

It generates a complete rangoli from a random seed: a layered center medallion, concentric rings filling the canvas, and an outer ring of diyas. It keeps your current floor. Not quite right? Tap it again — every tap is a brand-new design.

How do I save my design?

Tap Save to Gallery. The gallery keeps a thumbnail plus the full editable design — tap any saved piece to reload it onto the canvas, or long-press to delete it.

How do I share or export my rangoli?

Use Export PNG to render a 2048 × 2048 image — with the floor, or transparent for overlays. From the step-by-step guide you can also export the full illustrated instructions as a multi-page PDF.

Does Rangoli Studio need an internet connection?

No. Everything is drawn and stored on your device and the app works fully offline.

What data does Rangoli Studio collect?

None. No account, no analytics, no ads. See our Privacy Policy.
