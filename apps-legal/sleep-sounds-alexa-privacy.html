Privacy Policy — Slumber Sounds (Alexa Skill)

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Slumber Sounds is an Alexa skill published by Mushak Labs LLC that plays sleep sounds and pre-mixed sound scenes on Alexa-enabled devices. This policy describes what information the skill does and does not handle.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The skill has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics, and no in-skill purchases. We do not ask for or receive your name, email address, location, or payment information.

Playback state

To make pause and resume work, the skill's backend (hosted on Cloudflare) stores a small piece of functional state — the sound or scene you last played, your playback position, and your loop preference — keyed to the anonymous user identifier that Amazon assigns to your account for this skill. This state is used for nothing else, is never shared or sold, and expires automatically after 30 days.

Voice requests

Your voice requests are processed by Amazon Alexa under Amazon's own privacy policy. The skill receives only the intent of your request (for example, "play rain") and the identifiers Amazon includes with every skill request.

Audio

Audio files are served from Cloudflare storage. Standard server logs (such as IP addresses of requesting devices) may be generated by Cloudflare and Amazon in the ordinary course of delivering the service; we do not use them to identify or track individuals.

Children

The skill is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
