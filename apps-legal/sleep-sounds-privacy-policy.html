Privacy Policy for Sleep Sounds

Effective: September 28, 2026

Sleep Sounds does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works fully offline — your sound mixes and settings are saved only on your device and never leave it.

We use no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see, access, or share anything about you because there is nothing to see.

If you contact us at hello@mushaklabs.com with a question, we will only use your message to reply to you.
