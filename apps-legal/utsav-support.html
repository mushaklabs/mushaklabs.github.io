Frequently asked questions

A festival date differs from my local calendar by a day. Why?
Utsav follows North Indian (Smārta / pūrṇimānta) tradition. Regional observance can vary by a day.

How do reminders work?
Reminders are notifications scheduled on your device — one the evening before and one the morning of each festival you enable. Turn them on per festival or for all festivals in Settings. They work without internet.

How do I add a festival to my Apple Calendar?
Open any festival and tap the Calendar button. Utsav uses write-only access — it can add events but cannot read your calendar.

Does Utsav need an internet connection?
No. Everything — the festival data, countdown, reminders, and settings — lives on your device.

What data does Utsav collect?
None. No account, no analytics, no ads. See our Privacy Policy.
